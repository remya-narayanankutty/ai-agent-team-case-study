# A Measurement Framework for a Small Store

Remya Narayanankutty | Founder and CEO, Belong India

## The problem
A small store's dashboard can mislead. Total traffic included a large share of crawler and non-target traffic, and social and email clicks were not being attributed. I needed a few numbers I could trust, reviewed weekly with little effort.

## What I built
- **One primary metric.** Visits from the target market only, not total visits. Total traffic was inflated by bots and visitors outside the audience, so it was the wrong measure of progress.
- **A tagging standard.** Every link shared outside the store carries a consistent set of tracking tags, by channel: email, social bio link, stories, reels, messaging broadcasts. A sale email had gone out with no tags, so its clicks were counted as direct visits.
- **A weekly tracker.** A shared sheet with a dashboard, a list of quick wins, an action log, and a checkout health monitor.
- **A weekly loop.** Search data refreshes by itself. I copy in two numbers from the store. The AI reads the quick wins and makes the changes in the same session.

## How it works
- **Checkout health.** Days with visits and carts but no completed purchases usually point to a payment fault, not to demand, so they are monitored as their own line.

## What went wrong and what I changed
- Untagged email links were invisible. The tagging standard fixed that going forward.
- The first dashboard measured the wrong thing. Changing the headline metric changed every decision after it.

## How AI was used
- **Pattern:** A weekly human-and-AI loop. Data refreshes automatically, I copy in two numbers, and the AI analyses them and makes the changes in the same session.
- **Autonomy:** Low to medium. The AI recommends quick wins and applies the approved ones.
- **Guardrails:** One trusted headline metric, a standard tagging scheme for every external link, and a checkout health monitor.
- **Human checkpoints:** I choose which quick wins go ahead.
- **How it improved:** The first review changed the headline metric, and every later decision used the better measure.
- **My role:** I chose the metric and the weekly rhythm.

## Results
Reporting now takes minutes a week. Trends will be added as the series builds up and becomes meaningful.

## What I would do next
Automate the two manual copy-in steps.
